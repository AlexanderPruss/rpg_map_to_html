# Cell 007.006

## Title - Glamour House

What hides behind the illusion?

## Left Column

#### Basic information

Explored? Fully, except for the house.

Terrain - Dense woods.

### Glamour House

We found a clearing with a beautiful, welcoming farmstead. Before we approached, we realized that each of us saw the home differently; one of us saw it as a cabin, another as a longhouse, another as a small chateau. Other details were also different. While we were intrigued, we decided to investigate later, as we were tracking Cletus.

### Forgetting

As we left this area, we found our memory of what we'd seen quickly fading. We remembered how nice and welcoming the house was, but only in general terms.

## Right Column

#### Mechanical Birds

The one detail we all saw, and all agreed on, were the large number of mechanical birds in the farmstead. Chickens, robins, ravens, all of clockwork. While we were looking at the house, this seemed normal; the strangeness only became apparent once we left.
